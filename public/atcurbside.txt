The AtCurbside Stickers project was all about bringing the brand’s personality to life through creative and cohesive sticker designs. I worked on conceptualizing and designing custom stickers used for product packaging, promotions, and overall brand visibility for AtCurbside Coffee Roastery.

My goal was to create layouts that look visually appealing while still being practical for everyday use. Each design was carefully crafted to reflect the coffee shop’s identity, using consistent typography, color palettes, and graphic elements that stay true to the brand. I also made sure the stickers remained clear and readable across different sizes and worked well when printed and applied to real packaging.

Through this project, the stickers helped elevate product presentation, strengthen brand consistency, and create a more memorable and engaging experience for customers interacting with the brand.
