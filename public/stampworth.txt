Stampworth is a digital loyalty platform designed to modernize traditional stamp card systems by providing a practical and scalable solution for Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs). The project addresses common problems found in physical loyalty programs — such as lost cards, limited customer engagement, and limited use of customer data — by transforming the entire experience into a mobile-first ecosystem.

Currently under development as our final capstone project, Stampworth aims to create a complete digital loyalty solution that benefits both customers and business owners through two integrated mobile applications.

The Stampworth app allows customers to discover participating stores, monitor their points and rewards, and generate a unique QR code for seamless check-ins. Instead of collecting physical stamps, users receive digital stamps through quick QR scanning, making the loyalty experience more convenient and accessible.

On the business side, the Stampworth Business app serves as a management dashboard where merchants can create customized rewards, track redemption activity, and view real-time customer engagement data. This helps store owners better understand customer behavior while simplifying loyalty program management.

To go beyond simply digitizing traditional stamp cards, Stampworth integrates geofencing technology that enables proactive customer engagement. When users enter a predefined area near a participating store, the app can send personalized notifications such as welcome messages, reward reminders, or limited-time offers directly to their smartphones. This transforms the loyalty program into an active marketing channel that connects with customers at the right place and time.

By combining QR-code transactions with location-based engagement, Stampworth aims to help SMEs strengthen customer relationships, improve retention, and encourage repeat visits — ultimately turning occasional buyers into loyal customers in today’s increasingly digital marketplace.
